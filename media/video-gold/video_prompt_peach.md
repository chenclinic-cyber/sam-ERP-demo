# 水蜜桃版影片生成提示詞（Gemini / Veo 用）

依照洋蔥版的三幕結構改寫。生成後把影片傳回，即可套用
同樣的金色 Logo、金外套色調與 CTA 結尾卡後製。

## 中文提示（可直接貼給 Gemini）

10秒醫美診所廣告，3D擬人化水果角色，質感如皮克斯電影：

第1幕（0-3秒）：一顆擬人化的水蜜桃小姐，有著精緻的大眼睛、
長睫毛和豐唇，畫著粉金色眼影，披著金色毛茸茸的奢華皮草外套，
拎著黑色鏈條小包，優雅地推開高級醫美診所的玻璃門走進來。
診所內是大理石與金色裝潢，櫃檯有穿白袍的美麗接待人員微笑迎接。
但水蜜桃小姐的表皮看起來有點乾燥、失去光澤，還有些細紋。

第2幕（3-8秒）：治療室內，水蜜桃小姐戴著黑色護目鏡躺在治療椅上，
美麗的女醫師戴著藍色手套，用探頭為她進行水光煥膚療程。
鏡頭特寫：乾燥暗沉的表皮漸漸變得水潤透亮，
一半已恢復粉嫩多汁的蜜桃光澤，水珠在表面滾動。

第3幕（8-10秒）：水蜜桃小姐容光煥發地走出診所大門，
表皮粉嫩水潤、吹彈可破，自信地向鏡頭揮手，
金色毛草外套隨步伐輕擺，背景是高級診所外觀與街景。

風格：電影感打光、奢華金粉色調、淺景深、
高級醫美廣告質感。配上輕快優雅的背景音樂。

## English prompt (for Veo)

A 10-second luxury aesthetic clinic ad, Pixar-style 3D anthropomorphic
fruit character:

Scene 1 (0-3s): An elegant anthropomorphic peach lady with big glamorous
eyes, long lashes, full lips and rose-gold eyeshadow, wearing a fluffy
GOLD fur coat and carrying a small black chain purse, pushes open the
glass door of a high-end aesthetic clinic (marble and gold interior,
a beautiful receptionist in a white coat smiles behind the counter).
Her peach skin looks slightly dry, dull, with fine wrinkles.

Scene 2 (3-8s): In the treatment room, the peach lady wears black safety
goggles on a treatment chair while a beautiful female doctor in blue
gloves treats her with a skin-booster handpiece. Close-up: the dry dull
peach skin gradually turns dewy and radiant — half already restored to
juicy pink glow with water droplets rolling off.

Scene 3 (8-10s): The radiant peach lady walks out of the clinic doors,
skin plump, dewy and flawless, waving confidently at the camera, her
gold fur coat swaying, upscale clinic facade and street in background.

Style: cinematic lighting, luxurious gold-pink palette, shallow depth
of field, premium medical-aesthetic commercial look. Upbeat elegant
background music.
